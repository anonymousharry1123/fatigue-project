## Evaluation and prediction boundaries

AI-3's expanding replay and AI-4's frozen evaluation answer different software questions. AI-3 can use an earlier evaluation outcome after it becomes available for a later prediction. AI-4 fits personal B0, B1 and M1 **once** at a shared UTC cutoff, before development and final outcomes, and uses the same fitted artifacts throughout both later partitions. Candidate/comparator choices are fixed in the manifest, and final observations have a separate explicit entry point. There is no automatic best-model selection or promotion.

Final opening is one-use within an experiment instance. It does not prove that a person never inspected the fixture or prevent construction of another instance. Known training-source tombstones in later supplied partitions invalidate the frozen evaluation; an explicit invalidation hook supports other upstream erasure. The evaluator does not watch app/private files or implement production deletion.

The numerical outputs retain raw estimates and clipped 1–10 estimates without rounding before metrics. Own-set errors and coverage are separate from pairwise identical-set comparisons; participant/macro summaries do not make repeated days independent. Paired error series support later review. Uncertainty is explicitly not estimated pending a suitable statistical plan. An untouched synthetic partition is a test of software separation, not new empirical evidence.

The shadow harness uses an injected clock and simulated store receipts. Its records can test ordering, missed executions, missing outcomes and erasure races, but cannot establish native persistence or real prospective adherence. No shadow prediction appears in the check-in flow or drives Coach. Frozen artifacts remain synthetic and incompatible with the legacy 0–100 residual model.

The end-to-end command below passes the frozen synthetic M1 artifact through strict serialization into a four-day shadow calendar. It deliberately skips one morning, links later outcomes, checks equivalent reports after reopening the simulated store and checks that opt-out erases that store. These checks use in-memory state and invented clock times, not a real app process restart or an iOS file write.

## Ordinary collection procedure, when using the local plan

These are the current app's operational steps. They do not require enabling a prediction model, and this report has not performed them on a personal device. Decisions about the study's measurement meaning, participant-facing data use and intended scientific claims remain in the [prediction contract](PREDICTION_CONTRACT.md); the preparation audit adds no new approval flow to ordinary app use.

1. Use one issuing device and the intended existing/local session. In **Privacy center → Local study history**, explicitly enable **Keep local study history**, then open **Study tools**. Choose one source and a calendar length, and use **Start tomorrow's plan**. Manual is the default; the editable 28-day default is scheduling convenience, not sample-size advice. Existing records are not backfilled.
2. Keep the plan's source and timezone fixed. For manual sleep, save one genuine main-sleep entry ending that morning by 10:00. For Apple Health, connect and permit sleep reads beforehand, and allow readable data to reach the issuing device by cutoff. Late imports or records on another device do not establish earlier local availability. Naps and competing main episodes do not silently become the main-sleep feature.
3. Open Tonyo from 10:00 until before 10:15 for its morning feature snapshot, allowing the save to complete. The feature remains limited to information available by 10:00. Foreground minute checks/resume are implemented; a reliable background run is not promised, and an unopened/missed day stays in the denominator.
4. In English, save an **Evening** check-in from 18:00 until before 22:00. The study target needs Energy; other answers remain optional. An unanswered, late or subsequently revised selected target remains visible as missing/unusable in assessment. Do not manufacture an earlier response when a window was missed.
5. After windows close, use **Assess local readiness** for aggregate status. **Save private study export** is a separate confirmed file-picker action. Choose an on-device destination if the copy must remain local; OS cloud destinations may be offered. There is no Tonyo upload or clipboard fallback. Saved copies require separate deletion by their holder.

Check the local-history status after a capture error. Backward clocks, interrupted receipts, corruption or storage limits pause capture rather than establish uncertain availability. An observed timezone mismatch or relevant source deletion conservatively invalidates the plan lineage until clear/restart; turning history off clears the retained study history while preserving ordinary logs. Do not edit timestamps to repair an unavailable opportunity. Health queries reflect only readable data, cannot prove complete read authorization and cannot detect every old external change.

The [data dictionary](AI1_DATA_DICTIONARY.md) defines export-local aliases and exact tables. Independent files/devices cannot be joined by assuming equal pseudonyms identify the same person. No private study export belongs in this repository or in software-review prompts.
