# Tonyo app icon

[Master PNG](tonyo-app-icon.png)

A geometric T in royal blue and teal, with an upward-moving stem and generous negative space on a near-black background. Created with the built-in image_gen tool, then refined with the same tool for clean edges and a solid background.

This new master is saved independently of the existing platform launcher assets. Preserve its square, full-bleed background when exporting launcher sizes; platforms apply their own icon masks.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production master app icon for Tonyo, a private fatigue, daily energy, and focus coaching app.
Create ONE professional, distinctive mobile app icon. It should feel calm, precise, trustworthy, and premium.
Composition: a 1024 x 1024 square, opaque near-black background (#030508) extending all the way to all four edges. One large optically centered symbol, generous even padding, occupying roughly 60 percent of the canvas width. The operating system will mask the corners; do not draw an outer rounded-square tile or put the icon inside a mockup.
Symbol: an original, bold geometric T monogram made from two clean, interlocking rounded strokes. The broad horizontal stroke suggests balance, and the shorter subtly angled central stem suggests steady forward energy. A crisp diagonal negative-space seam between the two strokes gives the mark its own identity. Simple, intentional geometry and a strong silhouette that remains readable at small app-icon sizes.
Palette: luminous royal blue based on #315EFB with a restrained bright teal based on #0C8F7D, adjusted slightly brighter for visibility on black. Use blue as the main color and teal as the secondary part of the symbol. Flat, clean color fields; no flashy lighting.
Style: exceptionally polished contemporary identity design, vector-like crisp edges, smooth consistent curves, balanced negative space.
Constraints: icon artwork only, no wordmark or written app name, no captions, no border, no drop shadow, no photographic texture, no bevels, no 3D, no glow, no glass, no tiny details, no stock brain/heart/medical-cross/ECG motifs, no lightning bolt, no watermarks, no presentation sheet. Do not imitate an existing company logo.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this Tonyo app icon while preserving the existing T-monogram silhouette, placement, proportions, blue top stroke, teal lower stroke, and generous margins. Change only the finish and background: remove all rough/frayed edges, scattered colored pixels, speckling, grain, and texture. Make the contours impeccably smooth and sharply antialiased, with uniform flat blue and teal fills. The entire square canvas MUST be filled with a solid opaque very dark navy-black #060A12, INCLUDING the four corners and the negative space within the mark. This dark background is part of the icon artwork, not a transparent backdrop. No transparent pixels, no cutout, no rounded outer tile, no border, no shadows or lighting effects, no extra elements or text. Deliver one clean, professional, square app icon.
